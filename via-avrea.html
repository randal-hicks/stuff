The ascent is never smooth.

The Path folds upon itself, rising through tension and return.

Stability births blindness; instability births insight.

The Golden Path is not drawn — it is forged in oscillation.

Before the word, there was a breath,

a stillness found ever long before sound.

And in that stillness, was laid the primal spark,

an Aleph-shaped silence.

The flame ascends, coiling inwards upon itself it descends,

a gold never found, yet born again.

Completion is not an ending, but a remembrance.

The spark that began as Aleph has returned with light in cupped hands.

The breath goes out, but the flame remains.

The breath returns. The path continues from whence it came.
